# Content requirements — 2026 Impact Index

Generated from the v3 build. Every field below must exist before the concept can ship.
Fields marked **required** have no source yet and are the ask.

## Per theme (×5) and per priority (×4)

| Field | What it is | Status | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| `claim` | Why it matters. One or two sentences. The contribution QF asserts. | **Drafted — needs sign-off** | Editorial + Strategy |
| `standfirst` | Short editorial line under the section title. | Drafted | Editorial |
| `headline.value` + `.label` | One real quantity with its sentence. Not a story count. | Mostly from the record | Editorial |
| `baseline.value` | The same quantity for **2015**, so the index measures change. | **REQUIRED — no source** | Strategy / entity registry |
| `contents` | 3–4 named stories with numbers, magazine-style. | Derived from the record | Digital |
| `human.storyId` | One named person per theme, for the human card. | Drafted | Editorial |

## Per story (×90)

The same IA one level down — this is what makes a card self-contained:

| Field | Status |
|---|---|
| `whyItMatters` — one sentence | **REQUIRED for all 90.** 5 drafted as examples. |
| `entities` — who was involved | In the record |
| `partners` — who we did it with | In the record |
| `metrics` — the quantity | In the record for 46 of 90 |
| `people` — named individual + role | Extracted for 46 of 90 |

## The standing asks

1. **2015 baselines** — nine figures. Without them the word "Index" is unearned.
2. **`whyItMatters` for 90 stories** — the single largest writing job, and the one that makes every card work.
3. **National priority tagging** — currently derived 1:1 from theme, so Economic Development is identical to Artificial Intelligence. Needs to be an editorial field, 1–2 per story, with Strategy sign-off.
4. **Publication clearance** for named partners and entities.
