Year in Review — four concepts

The same 90 stories from 2025, re-read as a tagged record, shown three ways. Each concept answers the brief differently; the differences are structural, not cosmetic.

01

The report

A long scrolling report after the Anthropic Economic Index: a world map of QF's reach, an entity grid, a story explorer and a queryable data table. Strongest on evidence and completeness; weakest on narrative and emotion.

One screen per question

Four entry points — Themes, National Priorities, Across QF, With partners — each locked to a single viewport so a control and its result are never separated by a scroll. Tight and legible; light on editorial voice.

The editorial index

After the Patagonia progress report. A masthead with the scrolling wordmark, then per-section: a claim, a two-up of photograph and contribution, and a five-card carousel led by the recap. Themes read as a magazine; priorities read as evidence. Grids vary by what each theme actually contains.

Current · magazine + evidence registers · recap as a card Open → 04

The paged stack

Four full-screen sections — Recaps, National Priorities, Themes, Partners — that stack: each slides away on scroll to reveal the next, and snaps to the nearest section when you let go. Priorities and Themes are a list beside one big stat card; Themes carries each theme page's own gradient. Built from the Figma "Paged (v4)" frames.
